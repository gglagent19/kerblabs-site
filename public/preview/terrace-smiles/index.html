The team

Founder-led, family-built.

Terrace Smiles opened in 2014 because the founders — both Tampa-raised — were tired of dental offices that treated kids' visits and parents' visits like two separate businesses. So they built one office that does both, and trained one team to handle both with the same care.

Every dentist on staff has been here at least four years. No revolving door. The hygienist who cleaned your child's teeth last spring will be the one who cleans them this fall.
